## cyclone-nargis.jpg

- **Used for:** the cyclone story map's card in the story map overview
  (`src/content/story-maps/cyclone-exposure/config.json`, `hero.image`).
- **Shows:** Cyclone Nargis over the Bay of Bengal and Myanmar, 3 May 2008, natural colour,
  NASA Terra satellite (MODIS).
- **Source:** NASA Earth Observatory, "Cyclone Nargis" (image 19860):
  https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/cyclone-nargis-19860/
  Original file: `nargis_tmo_2008124_lrg.jpg` (5600 × 7200 px), downloaded 2026-10-21.
- **Licence:** NASA imagery is generally not subject to copyright in the US and may be used
  freely; NASA asks for a credit and no suggestion of endorsement.
- **Credit shown:** "Image: NASA Earth Observatory" (in `cyclone-exposure/text.md`,
  `## Image credit {imageCredit}`).
- **Changes:** cropped to the full width at y = 1750–4950 px (7:4 around the storm), scaled to
  1600 × 914 px, saved as JPEG (quality 82, about 220 KB).

## river-flood-irrawaddy.jpg

- **Used for:** the river flood story map's card in the story map overview
  (`src/content/story-maps/flood-exposure/config.json`, `hero.image`).
- **Shows:** the flooded Irrawaddy (Ayeyarwady) River in Myanmar, 3 August 2015, Landsat 8
  (OLI). NASA's false-colour composite: floodwater is bright blue, so the flood stands out
  from land and clouds.
- **Source:** NASA Earth Observatory, "Flood Waters on the Irrawaddy" (image 86394):
  https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/flood-waters-on-the-irrawaddy-86394
  Original file: `burmaflood_oli_2015215_comp_lrg.jpg` (3000 × 3000 px), downloaded 2026-10-21.
  Original credit: NASA Earth Observatory images by Joshua Stevens, using Landsat data from the
  U.S. Geological Survey.
- **Licence:** NASA imagery and Landsat data are generally not subject to copyright in the US
  and may be used freely; NASA asks for a credit and no suggestion of endorsement.
- **Credit shown:** "Image: NASA Earth Observatory" (in `flood-exposure/text.md`,
  `## Image credit {imageCredit}`).
- **Changes:** cropped to x = 1100–2600, y = 500–1357 px (7:4, the flooded river and its
  floodplain), kept at 1500 × 857 px, saved as JPEG (quality 82).
